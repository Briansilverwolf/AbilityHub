# AbilityHub

A platform for job seekers to upload CVs and become discoverable by businesses and opportunity seekers based on specific qualifications.

## Overview

AbilityHub addresses the inefficiencies in current job matching processes by:
- Allowing job seekers to upload CVs and create searchable profiles
- Enabling businesses and opportunity seekers to search for specific qualifications and get targeted candidate suggestions
- Reducing friction in the job matching process through proactive discovery

## Features

- CV upload and information extraction
- Profile creation and management
- Qualification-based search and matching
- Job categorization
- Profile insights and analytics
- Notification system
- Privacy controls

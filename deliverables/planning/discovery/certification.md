# Requirements Discovery Certification

## Summary of Discoveries
This document certifies that the requirements discovery process for the discovery process for the job/CV matching platform has been completed for the current scope.

## Artifacts Created
1. **stakeholders.md** - Identified job seekers, opportunity seekers, and businesses/hiring entities as primary stakeholders
2. **current-state.md** - Documented current manual job search process and pain points (time-consuming, low recognition, advertising burden, screening inefficiency)
3. **business-goals.md** - Defined goals: privacy-preserving matching, efficiency improvement, matching quality increase, accessibility enhancement
4. **story-cards/** - Five story cards capturing functionality:
       - STORY-001: Upload CV and create searchable profile (High importance, Medium risk)
       - STORY-002: Search for qualifications and get candidate suggestions (High importance, Medium risk)
       - STORY-003: Implement job categorization and CV upload limits (Medium importance, Low risk)
       - STORY-004: View profile insights and analytics (Medium importance, Low risk)
       - STORY-005: Receive notifications for profile views and interactions (Medium importance, Low risk)
5. **open-questions.md** - Captured unresolved questions about CV parsing, privacy, search ranking, etc., plus future enhancement considerations

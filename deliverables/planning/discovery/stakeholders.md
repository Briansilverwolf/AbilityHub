# Stakeholders

## Job Seekers
- **Role**: Individuals seeking employment who upload their CVs to the system
- **What they do**: Create and maintain professional profiles/CVs, upload them to the platform, potentially update qualifications/experience
- **How they're affected**: Gain visibility to opportunity seekers, receive targeted suggestions for matching opportunities, streamline job search process

## Opportunity Seekers
- **Role**: People or businesses looking for specific qualifications who get candidate suggestions
- **What they do**: Search for candidates based on specific qualifications, review suggested matches, initiate contact with potential candidates
- **How they're affected**: Access to targeted candidate suggestions, reduce time spent filtering unqualified applicants, improve matching efficiency

## Businesses/Hiring Entities
- **Role**: Organizations seeking particular conditions/qualifications who want to connect with qualified candidates
- **What they do**: Define qualification requirements, review candidate suggestions, engage with potential hires
- **How they're affected**: More efficient candidate sourcing, better quality matches based on specific qualifications, reduced recruitment cycle time

# Engagement and Privacy

## Purpose
Provides insights into profile engagement, delivers notifications for interactions, and ensures privacy-protected handling of sensitive information.

## Primary actors
Job Seeker, Business/Hiring Entity, Opportunity Seeker, System

## Trigger
Profile view event occurs or interaction (save/follow) is initiated by another user.

## Outcome
Job seeker receives visibility into their profile engagement while maintaining control over their privacy settings and personal information.

## Realized by (system side)
SYS-CAP-007 — Profile Analytics and Insights Support, SYS-CAP-008 — Notification Generation and Delivery Support, SYS-CAP-009 — Privacy-Protected Information Handling Support

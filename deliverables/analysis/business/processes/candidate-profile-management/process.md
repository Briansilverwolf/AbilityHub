# Candidate Profile Management

## Purpose
Manages the end-to-end lifecycle of candidate profiles, from CV upload and data extraction to ongoing profile maintenance and constraint enforcement.

## Primary actors
Job Seeker, System

## Trigger
Job Seeker initiates CV upload or requests profile update/modification.

## Outcome
Accurate, searchable candidate profiles with validated data that comply with system constraints.

## Realized by (system side)
SYS-CAP-001 — CV Ingestion and Profiling Support, SYS-CAP-002 — Profile Data Management Support, SYS-CAP-006 — CV Upload Constraint Enforcement Support

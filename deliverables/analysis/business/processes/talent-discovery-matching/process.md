# Talent Discovery and Matching

## Purpose
Enables businesses and opportunity seekers to search for qualified candidates, refine results, receive alerts, and organize opportunities through categorization.

## Primary actors
Business/Hiring Entity, Opportunity Seeker, System

## Trigger
Business or Opportunity Seeker initiates a search for qualifications or browses opportunities by category.

## Outcome
Qualified candidate suggestions presented with relevant insights, enabling efficient connection establishment.

## Realized by (system side)
SYS-CAP-003 — Qualification Search and Matching Support, SYS-CAP-004 — Search Refinement and Alerting Support, SYS-CAP-005 — Job Categorization System Support

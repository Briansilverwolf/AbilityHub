## BUS-NEED-001

id: BUS-NEED-001
type: business-need
title: AbilityHub platform for efficient professional opportunity matching

The AbilityHub platform addresses the inefficient discovery and matching process in the professional opportunity market, where both seekers (job seekers, opportunity seekers) and providers (businesses, hiring entities) experience high friction, wasted time, and poor signal-to-noise ratios due to manual, reactive approaches.

## Evidence from Current State Pain Points:
- Job seekers must search multiple sources and manually apply to each opportunity (time-consuming, low recognition chance)
- Businesses spend resources on job ads and outreach while receiving many unqualified applications requiring filtering (advertising overhead, screening burden)
- Opportunity seekers must check multiple platforms and manually filter irrelevant options (scattered search, manual filtering)
- Both parties experience delayed responses and miss time-sensitive opportunities due to search latency
- Core problems include inefficient discovery process, high friction in matching, poor signal-to-noise ratio, and lack of proactive visibility

## Alignment with Business Goals:
- Enables privacy-preserving matching without exposing sensitive information to untrusted parties
- Improves efficiency by reducing time and effort for seekers to find opportunities and providers to find candidates
- Increases matching quality by focusing on specific qualifications rather than keyword matching
- Enhances accessibility by making professional qualifications easily discoverable by those seeking them
